Meet Rachel

Home is a foundation, a sense of belonging, and a reflection of who we are.

Rachel's career in real estate is rooted in more than a decade of hands-on experience. Before becoming a licensed broker in Washington in 2017, she spent ten years as a real estate investor, designer and project manager for residential renovations, so she knows what it takes to see a property's potential and avoid costly mistakes.

Calm under pressure and skilled at negotiating, she treats every client with kindness, dignity and respect, whatever their budget, and guides them with clear communication even through the toughest markets. A recognized visual artist and former arts commissioner for the City of Sammamish, she brings fresh perspective and creative solutions to every move.
