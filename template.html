    Data sources and how scores work

    Each factor is scored 0–100 as a percentile across all 155 neighborhoods, so a 70 means the neighborhood does better than about 70% of the others on that factor. Your match score is the weighted average of the factor scores, using your slider settings as weights: a factor set to 8 counts twice as much as one set to 4, and a factor at 0 is ignored.

    FactorWhat we measureSource

    Neighborhood boundaries come from each city's GIS (Seattle Neighborhood Map Atlas, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond and Issaquah neighborhood maps). Sammamish and Mercer Island do not publish neighborhood maps, so their areas are approximate groupings of census tracts. Neighborhoods with fewer than 600 residents (mostly parks and industrial land) are left out. Census values are blended from tracts and block groups by population. This is a prototype; verify anything important with the original source.
