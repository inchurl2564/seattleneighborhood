Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, how it's used and shared, and the choices you have. It covers this site, including Neighborhood Match, the neighborhood guides and the What's My Home Worth? page.

Who I am

This site is operated by Rachel Yo, a real estate broker with Realogics Sotheby's International Realty, 15 Lake Street, Suite 200, Kirkland, WA 98033. In this policy, "I," "me" and "Rachel" mean Rachel Yo. I am responsible for the personal information described here.

What you share with me

This site only collects personal information when you choose to send it through a form: a contact form, an "Ask Rachel" form, or the home value consultation request. Depending on the form, this can include:

  • your name, email address and, if you provide them, your mobile phone number and home address
  • details you give about your home or search, such as home type, timeline, best times for a visit and your message
  • if you send your Neighborhood Match results, the budget, priorities, matches and saved neighborhoods listed under "What's included"
  • whether you agreed to be contacted and to receive text messages, the wording you agreed to, and the time you sent the form

Email, calls, texts and my client records

When you contact me by email, phone or text, or after you send a form, I keep our messages and my notes so I can follow up and serve you well. These may be stored in my email account, my phone and the client-management (CRM) tools I use to keep track of conversations, appointments and your preferences.

How I use your information

  • to answer your questions and respond to your requests
  • to schedule, prepare for and follow up on a home value consultation or showing
  • to send homes, market information and updates related to your search, if you've asked for them
  • to keep a record of your consent to be contacted and of your choices
  • to meet legal, brokerage and record-keeping requirements, and to protect against fraud or misuse

I don't sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Text message terms

  • Consent. I only text you if you checked the box agreeing to receive texts and gave a mobile number. Agreeing to texts is never required to work with me.
  • What I text about. Replies to your questions, scheduling and confirming appointments, reminders, and updates about your home search or sale.
  • Frequency and rates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply; your wireless provider can answer questions about your plan.
  • Stopping texts. Reply STOP at any time. You'll get one message confirming you've been unsubscribed, and then no more texts. To start again, opt in the same way you did the first time.
  • Help. Reply HELP, or email rachel.yo@rsir.com.
  • Delivery. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Your number stays private. Your mobile number and texting consent are never shared with or sold to anyone for their marketing, and phone numbers are never included in lists uploaded to advertising platforms.

Neighborhood Match stays on your device

Your Neighborhood Match answers, priorities, filters, matches and saved neighborhoods are stored only in your own web browser on your device, so they're there when you come back. They are not sent to me or anyone else unless you choose to send them with a form. To clear them, use "Start over" in Neighborhood Match or clear your browser's site data.

Cookies, advertising and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, advertising pixels, analytics or other tracking tools, and it doesn't build a profile of your visits or follow you to other websites. I don't sell personal information or share it for targeted ("cross-context behavioral") advertising, so there is nothing you need to opt out of. Browser signals such as Global Privacy Control are respected by default. If this ever changes, I'll update this policy first and give you a way to opt out.

Service providers

A few outside services help run the site and my business. Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Formspree delivers form submissions to my email. It receives the information you enter when you press Send.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may record basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Google Fonts supplies a typeface used on the Neighborhood Match page, so your browser requests it from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • My email, phone, text messaging and client-management (CRM) providers store our communications and my notes.

Links to other sites, such as photo credits, data sources and my Sotheby's profile, go to sites I don't control, with their own privacy policies.

When I share information

  • with Realogics Sotheby's International Realty, as needed to serve you and to meet brokerage supervision and record-keeping requirements
  • with people involved in your transaction, such as lenders, inspectors, escrow or other agents, when you ask me to or when it's needed to complete it
  • with my service providers described above
  • with professional advisors, such as attorneys or accountants, where needed
  • when required by law, legal process or a government request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of you, me or others
  • if my business is transferred, in which case the recipient must honor this policy

I never share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

How long I keep information

I keep contact information and our communications while we're working together and for a reasonable time afterward, so I can follow up and meet legal and brokerage record-keeping requirements. Transaction records are kept as long as the law or my brokerage requires. Your communication preferences are kept until you change them. When information is no longer needed, I delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information I have about you and give you a copy
  • correct information that's wrong or out of date
  • delete your information
  • stop contacting you, or stop texts or emails specifically

Email rachel.yo@rsir.com or call 425.658.5300. I may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and I'll respond within 45 days. You can have someone you authorize in writing make a request for you. You won't be treated differently for using these rights.

When I delete your information, I keep only a brief note that you asked, so I can keep honoring your request. If you disagree with how I've handled a request, reply with the subject line "Privacy request appeal" and I'll review it and respond in writing. Depending on where you live, your state's privacy law may give you additional rights, and I'll honor them.

Children

This site is intended for adults and isn't directed to anyone under 18. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and if I learn that I have, I'll delete it.

Security

The site uses a secure (HTTPS) connection, form submissions are sent securely, and I take reasonable steps to protect the information you share. No website, email or messaging service is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive financial information, such as account numbers, through the forms.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the site or my business handles personal information, I'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
