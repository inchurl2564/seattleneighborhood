Living in Madrona

Madrona is a mostly residential neighborhood in east Seattle, Washington. It is bounded on the east by Lake Washington; on the south by E. Cherry Street, beyond which is Leschi; on the west by Martin Luther King Jr. Way, beyond which is the Central District; and on the north by E. Howell Street, beyond which is Denny-Blaine. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Madrona is a neighborhood in Central Area, Seattle with about 4,516 residents. The typical home value is about $1.12M (-2.4% over the past year), and 63 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with about average crime for the area.
