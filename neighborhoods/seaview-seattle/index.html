Living in Seaview

Seaview is a neighborhood in West Seattle, Washington. Seaview is bordered by Puget Sound to the west, the Alki and Genesee neighborhoods to the north, Fairmount Park to the east, and Gatewood to the south. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Seaview is a neighborhood in West Seattle, Seattle with about 5,585 residents. The typical home value is about $898K (-0.3% over the past year), and 84 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
