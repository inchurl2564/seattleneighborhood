Living in Lawton Park

Lawton Park is part of the larger Magnolia area. Magnolia is the second largest neighborhood of Seattle, Washington, by area, located northwest of downtown. It occupies a hilly headland overlooking Puget Sound. Magnolia has been a part of the city since 1891. Much of the headland is taken up by Discovery Park, formerly the U.S. Army's Fort Lawton. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Lawton Park is a neighborhood in Magnolia, Seattle with about 9,743 residents. The typical home value is about $1.02M (+0.0% over the past year), and 153 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with about average crime for the area.
