Living in Newport

Newport is a neighborhood in Bellevue, Washington. It comprises the Newport Shores district along the Lake Washington shore, and the Newport Hills/Lake Heights areas east of Interstate 405. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Newport is a neighborhood in Bellevue with about 9,563 residents. The typical home value is about $1.36M (-4.4% over the past year), and 103 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is mostly car-dependent, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
