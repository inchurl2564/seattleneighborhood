Living in Finn Hill

Inglewood-Finn Hill was a census-designated place (CDP) in King County, Washington, United States. The population was 22,707 at the 2010 census. The community was largely annexed into neighboring Kirkland in 2011. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Finn Hill is a neighborhood in Kirkland with about 13,294 residents. The typical home value is about $1.09M (-4.9% over the past year), and 203 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is mostly car-dependent, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
