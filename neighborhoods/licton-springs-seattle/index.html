Living in Licton Springs

Licton Springs or North College Park is a neighborhood in the informal Northgate district of North Seattle. It is bounded by Interstate 5 to the east, beyond which is Maple Leaf neighborhood and the Northgate Mall; Aurora Avenue N to the west, beyond which is Greenwood; N 85th Street to the south, beyond which is Green Lake, and N Northgate Way to the north, beyond which is Haller Lake. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Licton Springs is a neighborhood in Northgate, Seattle with about 7,317 residents. The typical home value is about $744K (-1.6% over the past year), and 97 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
