Living in Windermere

Windermere is a residential neighborhood in northeastern Seattle, Washington, named after Windermere in England's Lake District. It is bounded on the north by Magnuson Park, part of Sand Point; on the northwest by Sand Point Way N.E., beyond which is Hawthorne Hills; on the southwest by Ivanhoe Place N.E., beyond which is Laurelhurst; and on the southeast by Lake Washington. The area has been a part of Seattle since 1910. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Windermere is a neighborhood in Northeast, Seattle with about 4,082 residents. The typical home value is about $1.39M (-1.3% over the past year), and 67 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is mostly car-dependent, with about average crime for the area.
