Living in University District

The University District is a neighborhood and a major district in central northeastern Seattle, Washington, comprising several distinct neighborhoods. The main campus of the University of Washington (UW) is located in the district, lending its name to both the district as well as University Way NE. The neighborhood lies north of Portage Bay and the Lake Washington Ship Canal and generally east of Interstate 5. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

University District is a neighborhood in Seattle with about 17,358 residents. The typical home value is about $979K (-1.8% over the past year), and 22 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
