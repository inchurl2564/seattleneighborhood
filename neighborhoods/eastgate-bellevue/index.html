Living in Eastgate

Eastgate is a neighborhood of Bellevue, Washington, United States. The population was 4,958 at the 2010 census. It was annexed by Bellevue in 2012. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Eastgate is a neighborhood in Bellevue with about 10,369 residents. The typical home value is about $1.28M (-4.3% over the past year), and 110 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with about average crime for the area.
