Living in Atlantic

Atlantic is a neighborhood in the Central District of Seattle, Washington, United States. It is the northernmost neighborhood in the Rainier Valley area, located between Mount Baker Ridge and Beacon Hill, and may also be considered part of South Seattle. It is home to the Judkins Park neighborhood. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Atlantic is a neighborhood in Central Area, Seattle with about 9,110 residents. The typical home value is about $743K (-3.4% over the past year), and 75 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
