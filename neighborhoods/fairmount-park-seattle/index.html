Living in Fairmount Park

The Fairmount Park is a neighborhood of West Seattle in Seattle, Washington. It runs southwest along both sides of Fauntleroy Way, from SW Graham Street in the south to SW Edmunds Street in the north. Neighborhood features include Fairmount Playfield, a city park, and Fairmount Park Elementary School, part of the Seattle Public Schools system. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Fairmount Park is a neighborhood in West Seattle, Seattle with about 6,921 residents. The typical home value is about $763K (-1.0% over the past year), and 95 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
