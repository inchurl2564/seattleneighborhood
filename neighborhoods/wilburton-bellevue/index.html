Living in Wilburton

Wilburton is a neighborhood in Bellevue, Washington, United States. It is situated to the east of downtown Bellevue, and to the west of the Crossroads and Lake Hills neighborhoods. Wilburton started out as an old logging camp, and was annexed by Bellevue in 1967. Currently, 3,790 people live in Wilburton. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Wilburton is a neighborhood in Bellevue with about 4,978 residents. The typical home value is about $1.55M (-3.2% over the past year), and 50 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
