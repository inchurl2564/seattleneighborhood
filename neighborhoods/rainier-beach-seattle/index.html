Living in Rainier Beach

Rainier Beach is a set of neighborhoods in Seattle, Washington, that are mostly residential. Also called Atlantic City, Rainier Beach can include Dunlap, Pritchard Island, and Rainier View neighborhoods. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Rainier Beach is a neighborhood in Rainier Valley, Seattle with about 7,040 residents. The typical home value is about $622K (-2.4% over the past year), and 57 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is mostly car-dependent, with about average crime for the area.
