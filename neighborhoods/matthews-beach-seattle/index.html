Living in Matthews Beach

Matthews Beach is a neighborhood in Seattle, Washington; it and Meadowbrook are the southern neighborhoods of the annexed township of Lake City (1954). Matthews Beach lies about 2 miles (3.2 km) northeast of the University of Washington, about 8 miles (13 km) northeast of Downtown. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Matthews Beach is a neighborhood in Lake City, Seattle with about 6,244 residents. The typical home value is about $1.04M (-2.1% over the past year), and 81 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is mostly car-dependent, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
