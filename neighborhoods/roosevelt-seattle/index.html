Living in Roosevelt

Roosevelt is a neighborhood in northern Seattle, Washington. Its main thoroughfare, originally 10th Avenue, was renamed Roosevelt Way upon Theodore Roosevelt's death in 1919. The neighborhood received the name as the result of a Community Club contest held eight years later, in 1927. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Roosevelt is a neighborhood in Northeast, Seattle with about 10,077 residents. The typical home value is about $992K (-0.6% over the past year), and 68 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
