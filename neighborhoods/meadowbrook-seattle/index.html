Living in Meadowbrook

Meadowbrook is a neighborhood in the Lake City district of Seattle, Washington. Meadowbrook is centered on open fields adjacent to the Community Center, Meadowbrook swimming pool, and Nathan Hale High School. It is bounded on the south by NE 95th Street and the Wedgwood neighborhood, on the north by NE 120th Street and Cedar Park, on the west by Lake City Way NE and Victory Heights, and on the east by 35th Avenue NE and Matthews Beach. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Meadowbrook is a neighborhood in Lake City, Seattle with about 4,308 residents. The typical home value is about $856K (-2.1% over the past year), and 23 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
