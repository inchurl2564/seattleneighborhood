Magnolia, Seattle

Living in Southeast Magnolia

Southeast Magnolia is part of the larger Magnolia area. Magnolia is the second largest neighborhood of Seattle, Washington, by area, located northwest of downtown. It occupies a hilly headland overlooking Puget Sound. Magnolia has been a part of the city since 1891. Much of the headland is taken up by Discovery Park, formerly the U.S. Army's Fort Lawton. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Southeast Magnolia is a neighborhood in Magnolia, Seattle with about 4,725 residents. The typical home value is about $1.22M (+0.1% over the past year), and 82 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with about average crime for the area.
