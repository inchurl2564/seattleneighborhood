Living in Mount Baker

Mount Baker is a neighborhood in southeastern Seattle, Washington. The neighborhood's name comes from the view of Mount Baker in Whatcom County, that is seen by looking north over Lake Washington. It is bounded by Lake Washington to the east, Interstate 90 and then Leschi to the north, Rainier Valley to the west, and Columbia City to the south. The neighborhood has a community club and a rowing team. It hosts Seattle's annual Seafair, which includes an airshow featuring the U.S. Navy Blue Angels, hydroplane races, a fireworks show, and other festivities. Franklin High School and Garfield High School serve this area. It is part of Seattle's South End. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Mount Baker is a neighborhood in Rainier Valley, Seattle with about 7,262 residents. The typical home value is about $909K (-3.2% over the past year), and 113 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
