Living in Harrison/Denny-Blaine

Denny-Blaine is a neighborhood in east central Seattle, Washington. It is bounded on the east by Lake Washington; on the south by E. Howell Street, beyond which is Madrona; on the west by 32nd Avenue, beyond which is Madison Valley; and on the north by Lake Washington Boulevard E., Hillside Drive E., and E. Prospect Street, beyond which are Washington Park and Madison Park. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Harrison/Denny-Blaine is a neighborhood in Central Area, Seattle with about 2,285 residents. The typical home value is about $1.42M (-1.0% over the past year), and 33 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with about average crime for the area.
