Living in Crossroads

Crossroads is a neighborhood in Bellevue, Washington. It is situated in the north and eastern portion of the city, lying south of Bellevue-Redmond Road, east of Bellevue's Wilburton neighborhood, and north of Lake Hills. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Crossroads is a neighborhood in Bellevue with about 12,868 residents. The typical home value is about $1.17M (-4.6% over the past year), and 52 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
