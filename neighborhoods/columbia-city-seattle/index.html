Living in Columbia City

Columbia City is a neighborhood in southeastern Seattle, Washington, within the city's Rainier Valley district. It has a landmark-protected historic business district and is one of the few Seattle neighborhoods with a long history of ethnic and income diversity. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Columbia City is a neighborhood in Rainier Valley, Seattle with about 15,692 residents. The typical home value is about $753K (-2.2% over the past year), and 137 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
