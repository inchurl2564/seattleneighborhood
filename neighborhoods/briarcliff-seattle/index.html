Living in Briarcliff

Briarcliff is a neighborhood in Seattle, Washington. The city's Department of Neighborhoods places Briarcliff on the west side of Magnolia, south of Discovery Park. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Briarcliff is a neighborhood in Magnolia, Seattle with about 5,834 residents. The typical home value is about $1.57M (+0.1% over the past year), and 103 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
