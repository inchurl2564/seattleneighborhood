Living in Green Lake

Green Lake is a neighborhood in north central Seattle, Washington. Its centerpiece is the lake and park after which it is named. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Green Lake is a neighborhood in North Central, Seattle with about 10,145 residents. The typical home value is about $889K (-1.0% over the past year), and 112 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with about average crime for the area.
