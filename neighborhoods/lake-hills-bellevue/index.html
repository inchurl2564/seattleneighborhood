Living in Lake Hills

Lake Hills is a neighborhood in Bellevue, Washington. It lies to the south of the Crossroads and north of the Eastgate neighborhoods. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Lake Hills is a neighborhood in Bellevue with about 20,013 residents. The typical home value is about $1.09M (-4.9% over the past year), and 194 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is somewhat walkable, with lower crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
