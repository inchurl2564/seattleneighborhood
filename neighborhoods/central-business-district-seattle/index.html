Downtown, Seattle

Living in Central Business District

Downtown is the central business district of Seattle, Washington. It is fairly compact compared with other city centers on the U.S. West Coast due to its geographical situation, being hemmed in on the north and east by hills, on the west by Elliott Bay, and on the south by reclaimed land that was once tidal flats. It is bounded on the north by Denny Way, beyond which are Lower Queen Anne, Seattle Center, and South Lake Union; on the east by Interstate 5, beyond which is Capitol Hill to the northeast and Central District to the east; on the south by S Dearborn Street, beyond which is Sodo; and on the west by Elliott Bay, a part of Puget Sound. (From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Central Business District is a neighborhood in Downtown, Seattle with about 6,697 residents. The typical home value is about $591K (-4.2% over the past year), and 21 homes sold in the last 12 months. It is very walkable, with higher crime than most neighborhoods in the area.
